# KindredConnect

[Deployed Web Application Link] (https://kindredcareconnect.onrender.com)

KindredConnect is a centralised community platform designed to bridge the gap between elderly residents,
local care homes, and volunteers.
It has three intended users:
- Seniors (living at home or in care homes who need assistance or social engagement)
- Care home managers (staff responsible for organising resident events and recruiting volunteers)
- Volunteers (community members looking for opportunities to offer their time and skills)
This app seeks to solve the problem of senior loneliness and limited access to community support. Care
homes often lack digital tools to recruit and manage local volunteers efficiently, leaving resources
underutilised. By offering a mobile-first, accessible interface, KindredConnect simplifies resource matching,
increases senior engagement, and empowers volunteers to contribute effectively to their local communities.
